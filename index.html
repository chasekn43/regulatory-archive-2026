Statutory Dispute Letter Template

📄 12 C.F.R. § 1026.13 Formal Billing Error Notice

Copy and email directly to resolutions@affirm.com and legal@affirm.com

Under 12 C.F.R. § 1026.13 and the CFPB's May 2024 Rule on Digital Accounts, sending this written notice requires the lender to acknowledge receipt within 30 days, pause collection on the disputed amount, and complete resolution within 2 billing cycles without issuing adverse credit reports. 

TO: Affirm, Inc. (resolutions@affirm.com, legal@affirm.com)
FROM: [Your Full Legal Name]
DATE: [Current Date]
LOAN ID / ACCOUNT #: [Your Affirm Loan ID]
DISPUTED AMOUNT: $[Disputed Dollar Amount]
MERCHANT NAME: [Merchant Name, e.g., Farfetch, Amazon, Target]
CARRIER TRACKING #: [Carrier Tracking Number, e.g., FedEx/UPS/DHL]

FORMAL NOTICE OF BILLING ERROR UNDER TRUTH IN LENDING ACT (12 C.F.R. § 1026.13)
AND CFPB INTERPRETIVE RULE ON BUY NOW, PAY LATER DIGITAL USER ACCOUNTS

Dear Affirm Resolutions Department:

This letter constitutes a formal written Billing Error Notice pursuant to 12 C.F.R. § 1026.13 (Regulation Z) and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's (CFPB) Interpretive Rule regarding Buy Now, Pay Later digital user accounts (89 FR 47068).

1. NATURE OF BILLING ERROR:
On [Date of Purchase], I originated the above-referenced loan. On [Date of Return], I returned the merchandise in full compliance with the merchant's return policy. Carrier tracking ([Tracking #]) confirms delivery to the merchant's intake facility on [Delivery Date].

2. STATUTORY TIME VIOLATION:
Under 12 C.F.R. § 1026.12(e), the creditor and merchant were required to transmit a credit memo within 7 business days of return receipt. To date, no credit has been applied, and Affirm continues to assess unauthorized payment schedules against my account.

3. MANDATORY STATUTORY OBLIGATIONS:
Pursuant to 12 C.F.R. § 1026.13(c), you are required to:
  a. Deliver written acknowledgment within 30 days of receiving this notice;
  b. Immediately pause all scheduled loan withdrawals, late fees, and finance charges relating to the disputed amount;
  c. Comply with 12 C.F.R. § 1026.13(d)(2) by refraining from issuing any adverse credit bureau reports during this pending dispute;
  d. Fully investigate and adjust my loan balance to $0.00 within two (2) complete billing cycles.

Please confirm receipt of this statutory notice and verify the immediate freeze on automated debit collections.

Sincerely,

[Your Full Name]
[Your Phone Number]
[Your Mailing Address]
