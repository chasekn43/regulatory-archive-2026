### **Chronological Record of Events**

#### **Phase I: Voluntary Prepayment & Perfect Payment Record**
* **April 4–13, 2026:** Consumer voluntarily paid $3,167.11 to zero out every active Affirm loan ahead of schedule. Zero missed payments in account history.

#### **Phase II: The Intrusion, Geographic Anomaly & Immediate Mitigation**
* **July 7, 2026 (11:53 AM CDT):** Unauthorized intrusion into Shop account. Malicious actor initiated Order PE270138 via Affirm credit facility ($104.63).
* **Geographic Discrepancy:** Consumer's verified billing profile is anchored exclusively to Monroe, Louisiana. The fraudulent order was routed to 81 Keever Court, San Jose, California (1,800 miles away).
* **July 7, 2026 (12:55 PM CDT) — Emergency 49-Minute Call & "Rest Assured" Assurance:** Consumer placed an emergency 49-minute call to Affirm Customer Support immediately after the fraud hit. Frontline agents told the consumer to "rest assured" they would not be charged, refused an administrative freeze due to "pending" transaction status, and instructed the consumer to call back after the status changed.
* **July 7, 2026 (3:36 PM & 4:38 PM CDT):** Formal written cancellation demands sent to merchant (Perfume Empire) via email and portal hours before physical carrier dispatch.
* **July 8, 2026 (3:23 AM CDT):** Perfume Empire issued an automated refusal to cancel or intercept the shipment (OnTrac Tracking #1LSDCR10011QF38), claiming it had "already processed."

#### **Phase III: Zero Outreach & Affirm's 86-Minute Automated Denial**
* **July 7–9, 2026 — Zero Outreach by Affirm:** Affirm performed zero follow-up or proactive outreach to the consumer after the pending transaction status cleared.
* **July 9, 2026 (11:47 AM CDT):** Affirm Customer Care (Agent "Jason") issued a written acknowledgment of suspected fraud, promising a 30-day investigation and pausing credit furnishing.
* **July 9, 2026 (12:21 PM & 2:15 PM CDT) — 27 Minutes of Follow-Up Calls & Reroutes:** Forced by Affirm's total silence, the consumer initiated multiple follow-up calls logging 27 minutes of call time and department reroutes (bringing total phone time across both days to 76 minutes) to force the fraud investigation forward.
* **July 9, 2026 (1:13 PM CDT — 86 Minutes Later):** Affirm's automated system dispatched a final denial closing the investigation without human review of IP logs, device fingerprints, or the 1,800-mile geographic discrepancy. Affirm demanded full liability for Loan #XQ8M-YX19.

#### **Phase IV: Law Enforcement & In-App Web Affidavit**
* **July 9, 2026:** Official Identity Theft Incident Report filed with the **Monroe Police Department (Case #26-29572)** following Affirm's 86-minute automated denial.
* **In-App Web Affidavit:** FTC Fraud Web Affidavit completed and submitted directly through Affirm's in-app portal alongside police report documentation.

#### **Phase V: Written Liability Clearance & Account Lockdown**
* **July 16, 2026:** Affirm Order Review team confirmed in writing that the consumer was **"NOT responsible"** for fraudulent loan #XQ8M-YX19.
* **Simultaneous Lockdown:** Affirm froze the entire user interface, disabling in-app payment functions for legitimate, performing loans.

#### **Phase VI: Executive Aliases & In-House Legal Escalation**
* **Executive Aliases:** Initial customer communications sent under first-name-only aliases (such as "Scott from Affirm") carried a suspected identification as **Scott Williams, Vice President of Client Success**, prior to in-house legal counsel appearing.
* **July 17, 2026 (12:11 PM CDT):** Affirm Managing Counsel **Andy Y. Chen** issued a formal Cease and Desist prohibiting the consumer from contacting any Affirm personnel ("strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated").
* **July 17, 2026 (5:32 PM CDT) — The "Disingenuous" Email:** Five hours after issuing his C&D, Mr. Chen emailed the consumer stating: *"There is no conflict in directives: calling (855) 423-3729 to restore your access is the only way to resolve your account freeze... Moreover, your disingenuous offer to 'remit payment securely through' me is not, and never has been, an available option... any delay resulting from your refusal to do so remains your complete responsibility, not Affirm's."*

#### **Phase VII: Bank BillPay Workaround & Proof Transmitted to Counsel**
* **July 20, 2026 (4:01 PM CDT):** Mr. Chen threatened a $1,185.52 counterclaim on performing loans — including Loan #W57T-CWET, which Affirm's own records confirm was already zeroed out by a merchant refund.
* **Morgan Lewis Intervention:** Affirm retained **Morgan Lewis & Bockius LLP** (Madison Marshall / Arjun Rao), whose counsel instructed that all future legal correspondence go through them.
* **July 22–23, 2026 — Bank BillPay Execution:** Consumer executed payment workaround via bank BillPay. Affirm received, accepted, and applied these payments on July 22 and July 23 — disproving Mr. Chen's claim that calling Customer Service was "the only way."
* **July 24, 2026 — Proof Transmitted to Morgan Lewis:** Consumer transmitted the processed BillPay payment receipts directly to Madison Marshall (Morgan Lewis) in an email reply on July 24, proving payment performance despite Affirm's app lockdown.

#### **Phase VIII: Post-Intervention Unsolicited Outreach**
* Despite Chen's binding C&D and Morgan Lewis's representation directive, Affirm continued sending unsolicited team member emails and marketing solicitations for $6,880 in new credit lines.
* Consumer forwarded unsolicited Affirm emails directly to Morgan Lewis, quoting their attorney's exact representation directive and demanding why Affirm's operational teams were violating counsel's orders.

#### **Phase IX: Federal Regulatory Filing, State AG Notice & SMS Texts**
* **July 31, 2026:** Affirm filed a CFPB response marking the case "Closed with explanation," omitting the C&D order, payment lock, contradictory directives, and countersuit threats. Consumer filed a formal rebuttal with the CFPB.
* **July 31, 2026 — State AG Notice:** Formal complaint documentation transmitted to the **California Attorney General's Office (Rob Bonta / Nick Akers)**.
* **August 1, 2026 — Automated SMS Collection Texts:** Affirm's automated system (`shortcode 58083`) sent direct text-to-pay prompts demanding instant charges to an un-funded Affirm Money account (`*9596`), contradicting Chen's directive that calling Customer Service was the "only way."
