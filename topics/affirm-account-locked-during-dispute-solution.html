1. The Predatory "UI Lockout" Architecture

When consumers escalate fraud disputes or file CFPB complaints against Affirm, Affirm's legal department frequently initiates an account-level software freeze. In our verified empirical case study, Affirm Managing Counsel Andy Chen instructed customer operations to lock the account UI on July 17, 2026.

This software lock disables all in-app and website payment mechanisms (debit cards, saved bank accounts, and Pay Now buttons) across the consumer's entire account profile—including completely separate, legitimate performing installment loans.

The Manufactured Default Trap:

While Affirm disables the user's ability to make payments through the app, Affirm's automated collections engine continues issuing daily late notices, assessing late penalties, and threatening adverse credit reporting to Experian and TransUnion. This creates an engineered dilemma designed to coerce the consumer into dropping their dispute.

2. The Solution: The External Bank BillPay ACH Bypass

If your Affirm account is locked and customer support refuses to unlock it, do NOT send checks or interact over the phone without a paper trail. Follow this verified protocol:

Step 1: Set Up Direct Online Bank BillPay

Log into your personal checking account (Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, or credit union) and navigate to the Online Bill Pay section.

Step 2: Add Affirm as a Biller

Add Affirm, Inc. as the payee using Affirm's official payment processing address (650 California St, 12th Fl, San Francisco, CA 94108) and enter your specific Loan ID or Account Number in the account field.

Step 3: Preserve Certified Federal Reserve ACH Traces

When your bank processes the BillPay transaction, request the certified ACH Trace Number and Proof of Delivery. Under federal banking law (Regulation E and NACHA rules), an ACH trace constitutes definitive legal proof of payment tender.

Step 4: Use Traces to Defeat Outside Counsel Collection Demands

When Affirm or outside counsel (such as Morgan, Lewis & Bockius LLP) attempts collection on the performing balance, serve them with the certified bank ACH clearing receipts. In our benchmark case, outside counsel collection efforts collapsed immediately upon receipt of these traces.
