It is deployed to suppress regulatory exposure, enforce mandatory arbitration waivers, and contain administrative paper trails.

2. Professional Responsibility & Ethics: California Rule 4.2 / 8.4

The interaction between corporate inside counsel and outside litigation counsel raises critical ethical considerations under the California Rules of Professional Conduct:

  • Rule 4.2 (Communication with Represented Persons): When Managing Counsel orders a consumer not to communicate with corporate staff but demands they call customer support lines, the consumer is trapped in a contradictory administrative loop.
  • Rule 8.4(c) (Misconduct - Dishonesty & Misrepresentation): Making written assertions to federal regulators (CFPB) alleging phantom external bank chargebacks when internal ledgers confirm direct bank ACH settlement is an affirmative violation of truthful disclosure duties.
