Dear Chase Kinslow: 
 
 
This correspondence is in response to your request, which was received by the 
California Department of Justice/Office of the Attorney General (DOJ) on August 10, 2026, in 
which you sought records pursuant to the Public Records Act as set forth in Government Code 
section 7920.000 et seq. 
 
Specifically, your request is stated as follows: 
 
Administrative Routing Data for PIU #1553638: All internal administrative routing logs, 
digital timestamps, and system notes indicating when the correspondence associated 
with Public Inquiry Unit (PIU) File #1553638 was received, which specific DOJ personnel 
or departments it was routed to, and the exact timestamp of when the file was marked 
closed or resolved. 
(Note: I am strictly seeking administrative processing and routing metadata to verify 
handling timelines, not substantive investigatory work product or legally privileged 
communications.) 
 
Aggregate Complaint Volume: The total aggregate number of consumer complaints, 
inquiries, or reports submitted to the California Department of Justice regarding "Affirm, 
Inc." (or "Affirm") between August 8, 2024, and August 8, 2026. 
 
Enforcement Metric Data: Of the total aggregate complaints identified in Request #2, 
the statistical number of those complaints that were forwarded to the Consumer 
Protection Section or resulted in a formal enforcement action.

Each and every category of you request seeks consumer complaint records or 
investigative files, and on that basis we must decline your request. Complaints and 
investigative records are confidential law enforcement records of the Attorney General. 
Government Code section 7923.600-7923.625 expressly exempts from disclosure investigatory 
and security files of the Attorney General including complaints about unlawful practices. (See 
Williams v. Superior Court (1993) 5 Cal.4th 337, 354.) This exemption includes internal 
communications within DOJ regarding the handling of consumer complaints, including the 
processing and timelines for such requests, the aggregate number of consumer complaints 
submitted to DOJ, and the enforcement metric data you requested. 
 
We hope the information we have provided is of assistance
